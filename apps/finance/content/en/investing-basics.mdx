# Investing Basics: Where to Start

Investing can feel overwhelming at first. There are stocks, bonds, index funds, ETFs, real estate — the list goes on. But the fundamentals are simpler than most people think.

## Start with Why

Before picking any investment, understand your goal:
- **Emergency fund?** Keep it in a high-yield savings account.
- **Retirement in 30 years?** Low-cost index funds are your friend.
- **Buying a house in 3 years?** Don't put it in stocks.

## The Basics

1. **Pay off high-interest debt first** — No investment reliably beats a 20% credit card APR.
2. **Invest in index funds** — Low cost, diversified, and historically effective.
3. **Be consistent** — Dollar-cost averaging works because it removes emotion.

Start small, stay consistent, and let compounding do the work.
